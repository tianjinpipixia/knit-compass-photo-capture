## Weekly MD conclusions

### FACT

1. Hairy/fuzzy autumn knit expanded materially in the latter half of the week. RIVE DROITE added multiple fur/fluffy/shaggy/hairy wool/cashmere-oriented items and La boutique BonBon added wool/cashmere hairy items.
2. Constructed feminine silhouettes remained visible: peplum, shaped shoulder, layered, lace-connected, asymmetric and back-detail knit forms were observed in SNIDEL, ZARA and ADAM ET ROPÉ.
3. Natural-fiber value signaling strengthened: wool, merino, lambswool, cashmere and animal-hair expressions became more explicit in the higher price bands while value brands continued synthetic feather/shaggy approaches.
4. Late-summer function did not disappear. UV, cool-touch, dry-touch, washable and anti-pilling claims remained present in domestic value/mid brands.
5. Promotion noise was large, especially PAL CLOSET, JUN and ZARA Special Prices. Ranking movement must not be interpreted as pure material demand without promotion context.

### INTERPRETATION

1. Develop hairy yarns as two separate MD families: lightweight/easy-care synthetic hair versus premium natural/animal hair.
2. Prioritize 12G–16G thin but shape-retaining knit structures for peplum, shoulder form, compact jacket/cardigan and layered silhouettes.
3. Continue an autumn-color + residual-summer-function program for September transition demand.
4. For PAL10, state transitions such as preorder → ranking exposure → coupon → launch/restock are more reliable than ranking movement alone when promotion intensity changes.

## Material-development priorities

1. **12G–16G constructed feminine** — peplum, shaped shoulder, compact jacket/cardigan, layered/back-detail. Validate PBT/stretch support, plating, milano/compact structures and edge stability.
2. **8G–12G two-track hairy yarns** — (A) lightweight synthetic feather/shaggy with washable/static/pilling control, (B) wool/cashmere/animal-hair with premium hand and appearance.
3. **3G–7G lightweight craft surface** — tape/lily/tube/loop/crochet/hand-knit look while controlling actual garment weight.
4. **~14G autumn-color + residual summer function** — brown/bordeaux/khaki/charcoal/navy with cool-touch, dry, UV, anti-pilling or easy-care where commercially justified.
